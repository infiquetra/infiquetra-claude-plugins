## Findings

Two findings were surfaced during self-review and fixed in the same PR before this gate; both are
recorded here for the durable trail rather than silently absorbed.

| # | Priority | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P1 | The gate relied on `evidence_ledger.latest().superseded_fail`, which hardcodes a literal `"FAIL"` sentinel. The real shipped producers never write that string (`/qa`: `ship`/`ship-with-deferred`/`no-ship`; `/code-review`: `clean`/`blocked`), so a real `no-ship`/`blocked` verdict would have compared unequal to `"FAIL"` and been silently treated as satisfied — defeating the gate's purpose. | FIXED — `closure_gate.py` now reads `evidence_ledger.history()` directly and classifies against its own closed vocabulary (`_FAIL_VERDICTS`/`_PASS_VERDICTS`, KTD7); an unrecognized verdict HALTs `unrecognized-verdict:<check_id>` rather than being assumed to pass. Six new tests cover the real vocab (commit `2db7cd5`). |
| 2 | P1 | A malformed `leaf_saga_id` or `check_id` (e.g. traversal-shaped, never caught by `OutcomeSpec.validate()` since `evidence` is an open pass-through map) raised `EvidenceLedgerError` uncaught out of `evaluate()`, crashing the reconcile loop instead of a clean HALT. | FIXED — `Store` resolution and the per-check evaluation loop are wrapped, surfacing `invalid-identity:<subplot_id>` (commit `df68037`). Two new tests cover this. |

No P2/P3 findings remain open. Residual, non-blocking notes (already recorded in the plan's Risks
