# Ideation (second opinion): /muse Command Design — Codex gpt-5.5 @ xhigh

> Independent run commissioned because the prior `/ideate` survivors appeared infected by
> saga-internal constraints (text-only-docs bias, dead-wiring rule, a feasibility filter the
> seed doc explicitly put out of frame). Codex was given the same 28-seed doc and asked to judge
> the three contested areas (visualization, third-party apps, methodology depth) independently —
> to confirm OR refute, not to flatter. Verbatim Parts A–E below.

## PART A — INDEPENDENT RANKED SURVIVORS

### 1. Critique-Banned Yes-And Field
**Confidence:** 97  
`/muse` must structurally ban critique during imagination, not merely ask the agent to “be nicer.” Doubts are captured into a quarantine parking lot, but the live interaction rule is additive: build, mutate, mirror, associate, and defer judgment.  
**Basis:** direct: seed pillars S1, S19.

### 2. Inchoate-Elicitation Partner
**Confidence:** 96  
The core user need is not “generate ideas”; it is helping a human surface the thing they almost mean. `/muse` should specialize in mirroring, metaphor, unfinished-sentence prompts, desire-five-whys, sensory language, and “what is this not?” work before it ever asks for a crisp idea.  
**Basis:** direct: S5; reasoned: stated “tip of the tongue” purpose.

### 3. First-Class Creative Method Engine
**Confidence:** 94  
The method library is not metadata. `/muse` should visibly perform named creative moves: SCAMPER, Synectics, Zwicky boxes, Six Hats, Disney method, random stimulus, future wheel, backcasting, lotus blossom, and visual mapping, with convergent methods walled off until exit.  
**Basis:** direct: method library, S3, S26, S27.

### 4. Dual Provocateur + Scribe/Librarian Loop
**Confidence:** 93  
The partner must both keep the imagination moving and continuously organize the residue. If those roles split, either the session becomes airy chat with no durable compounding, or clerical capture with no creative lift.  
**Basis:** direct: S2, S13, S14.

### 5. Durable Multi-Session Muse Thread
**Confidence:** 92  
A muse thread needs its own durable corpus: raw sessions, distilled seeds, wonder log, canvas, imports, and parking lot. `/ideate` should receive a graduated seed bundle, not be the only place where state exists.  
**Basis:** direct: S13, S14.

### 6. Living Visual Thinking Surface
**Confidence:** 91  
Visual/spatial thinking is v1-worthy because the human needs to see relationships, clusters, gaps, and shapes. JSON Canvas plus markdown-derived mind maps should be a live surface the human and agent manipulate, not a decorative export after the fact.  
**Basis:** direct: S7, S8; reasoned: cognition requirement differs from docs policy.

### 7. Three-Driver Adaptive Orchestration
**Confidence:** 89  
The partner should fluidly switch between agent-decides, agent-asks, and user-directs. This prevents both rigid pipelines and vague chat: the agent can choose a move, offer forks, or obey “make me a mind map now.”  
**Basis:** direct: S4.

### 8. Incubation, Resurfacing, and Cross-Thread Pollination
**Confidence:** 87  
A multi-day imagination tool should remember dormant fragments and periodically reintroduce them without forcing closure. Old seeds, engineering-journal learnings, and adjacent muse threads can become provocation fuel.  
**Basis:** direct: S22, S23.

### 9. Satellite Round-Trip Protocol for Third-Party Creative Apps
**Confidence:** 82  
NotebookLM, Google Docs/Drive, Gemini Canvas, MindNode, Excalidraw, Miro, and Figma should not be the source of truth, but excluding them from the creative loop is wrong. `/muse` needs an import/export protocol so the human can use real creative surfaces and fold their output back into the local spine.  
**Basis:** direct: S10, S11; reasoned: human creative workflow.

### 10. Mode Palette: Capture, Provoke, Fan Out, Visualize
**Confidence:** 81  
`/muse` should have recognizable session modes: silent scribe, yes-and partner, persona panel, random provocation, mass unfiltered variants, artifact-first, visual mapping, and HTML prototype. These modes let the human control energy and texture without entering critique.  
**Basis:** direct: S17, S20, S21, S24.

### 11. Muse Artifact Rubric
**Confidence:** 79  
A good muse artifact is not “the best idea.” It is a fertile record: clear seeds, visible relationships, preserved weirdness, method diversity, unresolved questions, quarantined doubts, and routable next steps.  
**Basis:** direct: S12; reasoned: critique-banned work still needs quality standards.

### 12. Lifecycle Consolidation: Replace Office-Hours, Route Forward
**Confidence:** 76  
`/muse` should absorb the useful tail of `/office-hours`: frame and route once something nameable appears. Keeping both as separate fuzzy front doors risks command confusion; a thin compatibility alias is enough if needed.  
**Basis:** direct: S15; repo context: `/office-hours` is frame-finding and route-only.

## PART B — THE METHOD ENGINE, CONCRETELY

The method engine is the behavioral core of `/muse`: a repertoire of named creative moves, selected and rotated according to session texture. It should not feel like “anything goes.” It should feel like a skilled creative partner choosing the next stretch.

First-class methods for solo human + AI:

| Family | First-class methods |
|---|---|
| Inchoate elicitation | reflective mirroring, forced metaphor, desire-five-whys, magic-wand question, incomplete sentences, “not that” contrast, sensory translation |
| Divergent generation | SCAMPER, Crazy 8s, random word, free association, mass variants, lotus blossom |
| Reframing | five-whys to How-Might-We, abstraction laddering, first principles, inversion |
| Lateral provocation | de Bono PO, Oblique Strategies-style prompts, wishful thinking, artifact-first ideation |
| Combination | Zwicky/morphological matrix, forced connections, Synectics, TRIZ contradictions, biomimicry |
| Perspective shift | Six Hats without Black Hat until exit, Disney method, persona panel, extreme users |
| Future/speculative | backcasting, future wheel, pre-parade, design fiction |
| Visual/spatial | mind maps, concept maps, storyboards, journey maps, rich pictures, JSON Canvas clustering |

Convergent techniques like NUF, impact-effort, dot voting, and affinity selection should be quarantined until the exit ritual. They are useful, but in `/muse` they are brakes.

Selection across the three drivers:

| Driver | How it works |
|---|---|
| Agent-decides | The agent detects session texture and applies a method: circling gets mirroring or desire-five-whys; one stuck seed gets SCAMPER; too much abstraction gets metaphor or visual mapping; too many fragments gets clustering; linearity gets random stimulus or Synectics. |
| Agent-asks | The agent offers a fork: “Do you want to go wider with SCAMPER, stranger with random stimulus, or spatial with a canvas cluster?” |
| User-directs | The user can say “silent scribe,” “give me Six Hats,” “make this a mind map,” “hit it with Synectics,” “100 variants,” or “turn this into a rough UI.” The agent obeys unless it would introduce critique. |

Concrete moves:

**Inchoate elicitation**

> User: “I want it to feel less dead. I don’t know what I mean.”  
> Muse: “Stay with ‘dead.’ Is it dead like a museum, a voicemail tree, a spreadsheet, or an abandoned mall? Don’t explain yet. Pick the closest wrong metaphor.”  
> User: “Abandoned mall.”  
> Muse: “Good. Then the missing quality may be signs of life, not more features. Finish this sentence: ‘I would know it was alive if…’”

That is not general chat. It is a named elicitation move turning vague affect into seed material.

**SCAMPER**

Seed: “A multi-session idea partner.”

| Operator | Muse move |
|---|---|
| Substitute | “What if typing is replaced by voice ramble capture?” |
| Combine | “What if the engineering journal becomes provocation fuel?” |
| Adapt | “What if spaced repetition resurfaces dormant seeds?” |
| Modify | “What if one seed produces 100 deliberately unfiltered mutations?” |
| Put to other use | “What if `/muse` handles naming, narratives, and life decisions too?” |
| Eliminate | “What if the AI only scribes and never suggests?” |
| Reverse | “What if the AI throws wild artifacts and the human mutates them?” |

**Synectics**

> Muse: “Treat this idea as an airport baggage system. What are the bags, belts, scanners, stuck suitcases, and lost-and-found?”  
> User: “The lost-and-found is all the ideas I wasn’t ready to justify.”  
> Muse: “That becomes a real component: a nonjudgmental lost-and-found where unready fragments remain retrievable.”

**Six Hats without premature critique**

In `/muse`, the Black Hat is replaced by a parking-lot capture. Green Hat expands, Yellow Hat notices attraction, Red Hat captures felt sense, White Hat captures facts without forcing proof, Blue Hat manages the method, and Black Hat waits for `/ideate`.

What makes this a methodology engine:

- Every session has an active method state: current move, why it was chosen, what it produced, and when to rotate.
- Seeds keep method ancestry: “generated by SCAMPER-Reverse,” “from Synectics airport analogy,” “from canvas cluster.”
- The agent has method cooldowns so it does not default to the same prompt shape.
- The integration ritual distills method outputs into durable artifacts.
- The engine has a hard negative rule: no selection/scoring/ranking until exit.
- The user can inspect or override the method, so the system is guided rather than mysterious.

## PART C — VISUALIZATION & THIRD-PARTY SURFACES, JUDGED HONESTLY

### i. Visual / Graphical Thinking Surface

**Verdict:** v1.

Not all of it needs to ship at full strength, but the visual surface itself belongs in v1. Cutting it would misunderstand the product. `/muse` is not just an archival command; it is a cognitive workspace for a human who may think spatially, relationally, and visually.

Minimum v1:

| Surface | Role |
|---|---|
| JSON Canvas | The living spatial map of the muse thread: seeds, clusters, links, doubts, imports, questions, and exits. |
| Markmap-style markdown mind map | Fast outline-to-map rendering from ordinary markdown. Useful for “show me the shape.” |
| Simple sketch/storyboard protocol | Text-backed storyboards or rich pictures when a seed is experiential. |
| HTML prototype mode | For UI/product/service ideas where a visible interface clarifies the thought. Should be on-demand, not always-on. |

The key is round-trip discipline. Every canvas node should point back to a seed id, note, session, or import. If the human moves clusters around, `/muse` reads the changed canvas and integrates the spatial decision back into `seeds.md` or `wonder-log.md`. Screenshots may be evidence, but JSON/markdown remains the source of truth.

D2/Kroki-style rendering is useful, but I would not make remote rendering a required v1 dependency. Keep the dialects as optional renderers. The core v1 need is not “many diagram engines”; it is a living, editable visual map.

### ii. Third-Party Creative Apps

**Verdict:** v1 as satellite protocol; deferred for deep automation; cut as mandatory source of truth.

The prior instinct to avoid making NotebookLM, Gemini Canvas, Miro, or Figma load-bearing is sound. The move to push all third-party creative apps out of v1 is not. Humans actually muse in these tools. `/muse` should meet that reality without becoming hostage to OAuth, product churn, or brittle UI automation.

V1 should include:

| App/surface | V1 role |
|---|---|
| Google Docs/Drive | Optional text corpus and sharing surface. Agent can export/import clean text when connector access exists. |
| NotebookLM | Human-operated generator. `/muse` exports a source pack; human works in NotebookLM; exported notes come back through Docs/Markdown/import. |
| Gemini Canvas | Human-operated visual/generative satellite; output returns through copy/export/import. |
| MindNode | Markdown/OPML mind-map satellite. Good fit because it can round-trip structured outlines. |
| Excalidraw | Useful if `.excalidraw` JSON can be imported/exported; otherwise screenshot-only evidence. |
| Miro/Figma/FigJam | Deferred for deeper integration. Useful visually, but weaker as v1 because structured round-trip and account/API setup become the work. |

The live session flow should look like this:

1. `/muse` creates a “source pack” from the local spine: selected seeds, questions, parking-lot doubts, and context.
2. The human takes it into NotebookLM, Gemini Canvas, MindNode, or another surface.
3. The human exports notes, outline, document text, image, JSON, OPML, or link.
4. `/muse` imports that into `docs/muse/<thread>/imports/`.
5. The integration ritual distills imports into seeds, updates the canvas, and logs provenance in `satellites.md`.

Deep automation for NotebookLM via unofficial tools should be a spike, not default v1. The reason is not lack of imagination; it is operational honesty. Brittle UI automation and ToS-gray reverse engineering should not be the foundation of a creative command. But a manual round-trip path absolutely belongs in v1.

## PART D — WHAT `/muse` IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

`/muse` is a durable imagination studio for one human and an AI partner: a critique-banned place where vague urges, weird fragments, visual clusters, metaphors, raw transcripts, imported app outputs, and named creative-method moves can compound over days until something finally becomes nameable. Its job is not to decide what is good; its job is to keep the fragile material alive, mutate it through real creative techniques, make it visible, remember it faithfully, quarantine doubts without obeying them, and eventually hand a seed bundle to `/ideate` when the work is ready to be judged.

## PART E — RECONCILIATION vs THE PRIOR PASS

### Prior Survivors

1. **Structural critique-ban:** agree-but-reframe. Correct, but “no scoring code paths” is too implementation-shaped; the real contract is yes-and behavior plus doubt quarantine.

2. **Text-only markdown spine; cut visual cluster; third-party opt-in spikes only:** disagree. Plain-text spine is correct; cutting visual cognition and third-party creative surfaces is the prior engine’s bias leaking through.

3. **No new artifact; typed handoff straight into `/ideate`:** disagree. `/muse` needs its own durable muse thread. `/ideate` should receive a graduated seed bundle, not be the first durable artifact.

4. **Do not replace `/office-hours`; sit upstream as critique-banned opposite:** disagree. `/muse` should absorb the useful route-and-frame exit. A thin `/office-hours` alias may remain, but two fuzzy front doors is avoidable confusion.

5. **Incubation engine:** agree. Multi-session resurfacing is central, not a nice-to-have.

6. **Journal-as-fuel + auto-seed:** agree-but-reframe. Useful, but the primary fuel is the muse corpus itself; engineering journals are one provocation source among many.

7. **Data-driven method engine registry + silent technique selection:** agree-but-reframe. A registry is useful plumbing. The survivor should have been the creative method engine itself, with visible named moves and user-directed control.

### Contested Cuts

**Visual / graphical thinking surfaces:** artifact of prior-engine bias. The documentation decision against extra diagram dependencies does not transfer to a human imagination workspace. `/muse` needs a visual surface.

**NotebookLM and third-party creative apps:** mixed. Deferring deep automation is sound; deferring the satellite workflow entirely is bias. V1 should include round-trip protocols and at least low-friction import/export.

**Methodology library demoted to registry:** artifact of prior-engine bias. The 70-technique library is not decorative research. It is the substance of the partner’s craft.

### Net Verdict

The prior pass was right about critique-ban, parking-lot discipline, plain-text as source of truth, incubation, and the need for a clean handoff to `/ideate`. It went wrong by optimizing for Saga’s existing text-first documentation convenience instead of the human’s creative cognition. Its strongest limitation was treating visual tools, third-party creative apps, and creative methodologies as implementation risk rather than as core product surface.
