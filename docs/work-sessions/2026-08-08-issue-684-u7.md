## Key decisions

- **Delete vs shim the broker:** Delete whole. A deprecated shim importing `lease_broker` would have required keeping the 4,731-line module alive and would have contradicted R1/R2 (“no file under `plugins/` reaches the broker”). The breaking `3.0.0` for `team-execution` and `0.24.0` for `fleet-core` are the scope-consistent costs — keeping a shim to avoid the bump would have violated the re-key.
- **Guard must scan resolved paths, not just the tree:** A tree-only `grep plugins/` would have missed #642's rung-3 resurrection from `~/.claude/plugins/installed_plugins.json` and the cache-sibling scan. The guard's `_shim_resolved_paths()` loads `fleet_commons_shim` and `plugin_resolution` via `importlib.util.spec_from_file_location` and scans those roots if they exist — pinned by the fake-root test.
- **Frozen strings stay frozen:** `outcome-cross-runtime`'s `same_clone_coordination` and `fleet-broker-fencing` capability stay byte-equal in `outcome_compat.py:81`/`72` even after the broker is gone. Changing them would be a wire-protocol bump (golden fixtures at `tests/fixtures/outcome-cross-runtime/v1/` would drift). The docs carry the retirement note instead of the code.
- **Boot_id helper fix belongs in the test, not in production liveness:** Production already uses `_current_boot_id()` in both `bind_identity()` and `poll()` (P2 fix). The helper `_identity()` hard-coded `"boot-1"` while production on Linux returned a real uuid — the mismatch was test-prod drift, not a prod bug. Fixing the helper to call `LP._current_boot_id()` aligns the test with production without weakening the boot-id check.
